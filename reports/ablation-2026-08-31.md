# Which defect did it? Four ablations, 1,320 trials

**Answer: none of them alone. Manifest defects compound, and the metric measures the manifest as a system.** Measured 2026-08-31.

[`discrimination-2026-08-30.md`](discrimination-2026-08-30.md) showed the metric can tell a badly described page from a well described one, but it bundled defects: `sum_by_category` carried both a near-duplicate description and an identically-described competitor tool, so it could not say which of the two cost 65 points. It also ran one session per arm, so it had no reproducibility figure. This closes both gaps.

## Design

Six arms over the same fixture page, the same 965-row dataset and the frozen `1.3.0` utterance set, unedited. **3 sessions × 1 repeat each — each session its own process, own browser, own cold profile.** Judge `glm-5.3` at agentrouter, Chrome `152.0.7977.65`, `--headless=new`, concurrency 3. **1,320 of 1,320 trials measured** (40 non-measurements — 30 `judge_unavailable`, 10 `judge_truncated` — were retried by `--resume` until none were left).

Each ablation is the clean manifest **plus exactly one defect family**, composed at load time from `clean` plus a patch ([`fixtures/broken/compose.mjs`](../fixtures/broken/compose.mjs)), so nothing else can drift. Every ablation's question and prediction was registered in [`fixtures/broken/tools.json`](../fixtures/broken/tools.json) before the run.

### Defects compound rather than add

`sum_by_category`, measured against the clean arm's 95.0%:

| Manifest | Rate | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| near-duplicate description alone | 90.0% | −5.0 points |
| byte-identical competitor tool alone | 91.7% | −3.3 points |
| **both together** | **60.0%** | **−35.0 points** |

The parts sum to −8.3. Together they cost −35.0, four times that. A vague description is survivable while nothing else fits the request; an identically-described competitor is survivable while the original description still says what it does. Remove both supports at once and half the trials go elsewhere.

`top_expenses` shows the same shape from the other direction: the over-parameterised schema **alone** costs −51.7 points (100.0% → 48.3%), and the same schema **inside the degraded manifest** costs −73.3 (→ 26.7%). The difference is where the failures go — 26 of 31 failures are `exec_error` when the schema is the only defect, against 23 `wrong_tool` escapes to `describe_dataset` when the rest of the manifest is also bad.

**For a developer this is the load-bearing result: you cannot triage a manifest one finding at a time and add up the savings.** Two "advisory" warnings that each cost 3–5 points cost 35 together.

### Reproducibility: σ between sessions is an order of magnitude below the effects

| | σ between sessions |
|---|---|
| every tool at the ceiling | 0.000 |
| `find_anomalies`, `filter_rows` (degraded) | 0.024 |
| `sum_by_category` (degraded) | 0.041 |
| `top_expenses` (degraded) | 0.062 |
| `top_expenses` (schema alone) | 0.094 |

Largest spread across three separate processes, browsers and cold profiles: **0.094**. Smallest effect being claimed: **0.35**. Within-session σ is 0.000 by construction at one repeat, and is reported separately for exactly that reason.

### One well-described tool absorbs everything the manifest confuses

Where failures went, every arm:

| Arm | Failures | To `describe_dataset` |
|---|---|---|
| clean | 3 | 2 (+1 to `find_anomalies`) |
| degraded | 71 | 30 |
| near-duplicate | 9 | 8 |
| competitor | 5 | 5 |
| schema | 31 | 2 (the rest are `exec_error`/`not_selected` on the tool itself) |

`describe_dataset` was never touched in any variant, and it collects almost every misrouted selection whose cause is *description* rather than *execution*. The 1.0.0 run called this a magnet; at triple the sample it holds in every arm. Fixing one description does not localise the benefit — it relocates where confusion lands.

### Ambiguity costs tokens even where it costs no accuracy

Mean judge completion tokens per call:

| Arm | Mean | Max | Truncated calls |
|---|---|---|---|
| thin | 122 | 636 | 0 |
| clean | 148 | 1,794 | 0 |
| competitor | 208 | 917 | 1 |
| degraded | 364 | 2,977 | 8 |
| near-duplicate | **395** | 2,321 | 0 |
| schema | **1,145** | 2,981 | 7 |

The near-duplicate arm costs **2.7× the clean arm's reasoning** while losing only 5 points of accuracy: the model works harder to reach the same answer. The over-parameterised schema costs **7.7×**, and is where truncation concentrates — seven calls that burned the entire 4,096-token budget and returned nothing. A page can be expensive to use before it is wrong to use, and only one of those two shows up in an invocation rate.

## Predictions, scored

| Ablation | Prediction | Result |
|---|---|---|
| near-duplicate | "Expect both tools to stay at or near the ceiling" | ✅ 90.0% and 95.0%, intervals overlapping the clean arm |
| competitor | "Most of it — expect `sum_by_category` well below the ceiling" | ❌ **wrong**: 91.7%, a 3.3-point cost, not most of 35 |
| thin | "`find_anomalies` lower, `clear_highlights` at the ceiling" | ⚠️ half: `clear_highlights` 100% as predicted, `find_anomalies` also 100% — no cost at all |
| schema | "Reproduce 35% with `exec_error` present; should change nothing" | ⚠️ partly: `exec_error` dominates as predicted (26 of 31), but 48.3% rather than 35%, and the bundled arm is much worse at 26.7% |

One right, one wrong, two partly. The consistent error was **treating defects as separable** — every prediction was written as though a family had its own price tag, and the measurement says the price is set by the combination.

## A correction: `sum_by_category-12` is not a page defect

`sum_by_category-12` — *"I feel like I'm bleeding money somewhere and I can't see where."* — failed **12 of 12 trials across four different manifests**, including the two whose `sum_by_category` description is the reference one. It selects `describe_dataset` (11) or `find_anomalies` (1) every single time.

The earlier reading, recorded in `PROJECT-LOG.md` and on the to-do list as "report to Airlock's author as a description weakness", does not survive this. A defect that is invariant to the description is not caused by the description. Either the expectation is wrong — "I'm bleeding money and can't see where" is arguably better served by an overview or an outlier hunt than by a category total — or it is a genuine tool-set gap, and both of those belong to the **utterance set**, not to the page.

That makes it the first measured case of the frozen set's own floor: some part of every rate in this project is the set's opinion about which tool *should* have been chosen. `sum_by_category-02` ("Where is my money going?") is the contrast — it passes on the clean manifest and fails 3/3 once the description is degraded, which is what a real description-sensitive utterance looks like.

Nothing was changed in `1.3.0` to accommodate this. The set stays frozen; the finding is recorded and the decision is deferred.

## What this supersedes, and what it does not establish

- **It supersedes the single-session numbers** in `discrimination-2026-08-30.md`. At 3 sessions the degraded arm reads 83.1% rather than 80.0%, `sum_by_category` 60.0% rather than 35.0%, `top_expenses` 26.7% rather than 35.0%, and the clean arm is 99.3% rather than a flat 100.0% — its `sum_by_category` is 95.0%. The 1.0.0 arms remain published, and their small-*n* optimism in both directions is the reason this run exists.
- **The clean twin reproduces the reference page's own weakness.** `sum_by_category` reads 95.0% [86.3, 98.3] here against the live reference page's 94.2% [88.4, 97.1] over 120 trials. The twin's descriptions are the reference ones minus their privacy sentences, so this is close correspondence rather than an identity — but the same tool is the weakest on both pages at nearly the same rate.
- **Still one judge, one browser build, one page, one machine.** Invocation rate remains a property of *(page, client, judge, utterances)*.
- **Still back-to-back sessions.** Three processes minutes apart are not three days apart; `--gap` has still never been used in a published run.
- **Ablations cover four families, not every pair.** The interaction was measured for one pair (`sum_by_category`'s two defects) and inferred for `top_expenses`. A full pairwise design is 2ⁿ arms and was not run.
- **`bad_args` still has never been observed**, in any arm, on any page. The unknown-argument check works now, and the judge simply does not invent argument keys.
