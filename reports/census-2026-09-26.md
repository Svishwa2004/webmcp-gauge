# The cohort census — full capture, 2026-09-25/26

**The question:** of every submission to the WebMCP Challenge, how many actually shipped WebMCP tools — and what can an agent call on them that no page surface lists?

**The sample label, retired:** this is the **complete listing** — all 2,474 submissions in the gallery, 2,185 with a usable demo link, **2,183 capture targets** after alias collapsing. It supersedes [`census-pilot-2026-09-25.md`](census-pilot-2026-09-25.md), whose rates stay published there with the pointer added here; the pilot was the first 523 listed, a convenience sample, and the difference between its numbers and these is itself a finding (below). Nothing in this document quotes another project's description or names a third-party origin — every published field is a field of `publishable.json` (names, counts, lengths, booleans); descriptions and origins stay in the local snapshot, per the rules fixed before the capture ran.

## Provenance

- Captured **2026-09-25T15:29:38Z → 2026-09-26T04:45:47Z** (~13.3 h wall clock) in three recoverable parts: Chrome died once (1,134/2,183 saved) and the machine slept once (357 more saved); each failure cost exactly the one in-flight target, by the append-per-record design. The `--resume` and loud-browser-death handling this earned landed mid-run (inert to the running process) as commit `9dd3287`.
- One headless Chrome/154.0.8037.57, one page at a time, 2 s between projects, the harness UA on every request, robots.txt honoured.
- The agent view was read at the **browser endpoint** with recursive auto-attach — the item-23 fix, rehearsed that morning against the `spec-227` delegating fixture. A host-attached watch cannot hear a cross-site embed's registrations; the third-party numbers below are measurements that capture would have structurally missed.
- Reconciled: `probes/cohort-recompute.mjs` re-derived all 13 census fields from the 2,183 records and corrected the runner's part-3-only summary (12 fields disagreed, printed, rewritten).
- Dead-record composition, checked rather than assumed: 317 unreached = **215 network-unreachable hosts** (Chrome's error-page signature: title is the bare host, no document response ever arrived, zero tools), 47 robots.txt refusals, 36 HTTP ≥ 400, 10 navigation timeouts with no status, 9 capture errors.

| **Using WebMCP** (page registered ≥ 1 tool) | **1,507 of 1,866 = 80.8%** [78.9, 82.5] (Wilson 95%, n=1,866) |

Four of five reachable pages register tools. The median page ships seven. The largest manifest is 154 tools, and the browser offers an agent all 154 — the per-page budget question (the reported 296-tool ceiling that does not reproduce) now has a real-data point an order of magnitude short of it.

## What an agent can call that no page lists

**806 of 1,836 agent-view pages (43.9%) carry cross-origin delegated tools** — registrations that reach the browser, and therefore an agent, while appearing in nobody's `getTools()`.

- **799 pages (43.5%)** embed the same three-tool auth suite (`auth-get-current-user`, `auth-login`, `auth-signup`); **647 (35.2%)** additionally embed a five-tool site-agent suite (`ariag-frontpage`, `ariag-login`, `ariag-navigate`, `ariag-search`, `ariag-summarise`). Both suites trace to two third-party origins, kept local per the fourth-party rule; per-tool attribution lives in the local snapshot.
- 151 pages embed *exactly* the auth suite and nothing else third-party; **146 pages registered zero tools themselves and ship entirely through an embed** (8.0% of agent-view pages).
- 1,032 pages (56.2%) have purely first-party agent-visible tools.
- 40 records captured with live out-of-process iframe sessions — the registration path item 23 was about, observed in the wild.

**The pilot's bias, now measured:** the pilot slice read **74.9%** divergent; the full corpus reads **43.9%**. The shared suites concentrate heavily in the first-listed gallery pages — the convenience sample was not just incomplete, it was *directionally* wrong about delegation, overstating it by 31 points. Any pilot-derived claim in that write-up should be read through this one.

## Known residuals, recorded rather than smoothed

- **One mirror-image undercount** (1 of 1,836, 0.05%): one page reports 12 registered tools of which 5 never arrived at the browser union (no OOPIF session attached; the known late-enable non-replay mechanism). The pilot's separate capture had caught the same shape on a different page. Agent-side totals are best read as a **floor**; the divergence fields and `browserView` metadata expose every instance.
- 30 reachable pages have null agent views (navigation timeout before the watch saw anything) — nulls, not zeroes.
- The 13.3-hour capture window spans three parts; every target was visited exactly once, so no double-capture can inflate any count.

Entirely clean: **431 of 1,507 (28.6%)**. The pilot's readings hold at full scale: the schema family, not the description family, is what real manifests trip over (half of all adopting manifests require an undocumented parameter); and the fourteenth rule fires on **4.0%** (pilot: 3.8%) — 147 distinct pairs, including the textbook one (three byte-identical descriptions distinguished only by a team prefix). `budget/headroom` met its first real manifests: four pages at or past the break-at threshold, none of which refused to register — consistent with the 507-tool no-ceiling measurement. The reading for item 22's promotion decision is unchanged and now rests on the full corpus: **keep the warning**; promotion to error would fail four percent of real manifests on mostly prefix-heavy namespaces whose descriptions differentiate semantically.

## Reproducing

```
node probes/gallery-harvest.mjs --out=artifacts/gallery-2026-09-24 --resume
node probes/cohort-snapshot.mjs --targets=fixtures/cohort/gallery-2026-09-25.json --out=artifacts/cohort-full-2026-09-25 --delay=2000 --resume
node probes/cohort-recompute.mjs --snapshot=artifacts/cohort-full-2026-09-25/snapshot.jsonl
node probes/lint-cohort.mjs --snapshot=artifacts/cohort-full-2026-09-25/snapshot.jsonl
```

The raw manifests stay local by design; every number above is re-derived by re-running, not by re-analysing published rows.
