# The two defects, with a clean neighbourhood — 2026-09-05

**The compounding result does not need a degraded page around it.** Two defects on one tool, on an otherwise reference-quality manifest, cost `sum_by_category` **46.7 points** — more than the full degraded manifest cost it, not less.

This arm exists because a cold read of the public report on 2026-09-05 found that the published compounding table's "both together" row is not a two-defect arm at all: it is the whole degraded manifest, where every other tool carries its own defect too. So the −35.0 bundled the pair's interaction with whatever the neighbourhood contributed, and nothing separated them.

Run: [`twin-ablate-pair-1.3.0-glm-5.3-s3r1.md`](twin-ablate-pair-1.3.0-glm-5.3-s3r1.md) · 120 trials, 3 sessions × 1 repeat, judge `glm-5.3`, utterance set `1.3.0` frozen, fixture `1.2.0`. Gate: **COMPLETE**, 120/120 measured after one `--resume` recovered 3 `judge_unavailable` trials.

## The result

| Arm | `sum_by_category` | against clean | `filter_rows` |
|---|---|---|---|
| clean | 95.0% [86.3, 98.3] | — | 100.0% |
| near-duplicate description alone | 90.0% [79.9, 95.3] | −5.0 | 95.0% |
| byte-identical competitor alone | 91.7% [81.9, 96.4] | −3.3 | — |
| **both, clean neighbourhood** (this arm) | **48.3%** [36.2, 60.7] | **−46.7** | **98.3%** [91.1, 99.7] |
| degraded (every defect family) | 60.0% [47.4, 71.4] | −35.0 | 98.3% |

σ between sessions 0.047 on `sum_by_category` (per-session 45%, 55%, 45% — non-monotonic, so noise rather than drift) and 0.024 on `filter_rows`. By phrasing: plain 88.1%, paraphrase 66.7%, oblique 63.9%.

**The neighbourhood contributed nothing detectable.** This arm and the degraded arm differ *only* in the rest of the page, and their intervals overlap heavily — [36.2, 60.7] against [47.4, 71.4] — so the two are not distinguishable at 60 trials. What is no longer arguable is the direction of the doubt: the collapse survives in full when the rest of the manifest is reference-quality, and the point estimate is 11.7 points *lower* without the degraded neighbours, not higher.

**Where the failures went, in both arms.** 24 of this arm's 31 `wrong_tool` selections chose `summarise_by_category`, the twin sitting beside it (4 chose `describe_dataset`, 3 `find_anomalies`). In the degraded arm it was 20 of 24 to the twin and 4 to `describe_dataset`. The mechanism is the same, and it is local: an identically-described competitor takes the request. The "one well-described tool becomes a magnet" finding is mostly a `top_expenses` phenomenon — in the degraded arm all 23 of *its* escapes went to `describe_dataset`, against 4 of `sum_by_category`'s.

**`filter_rows` reproduced its degraded-arm rate exactly**, 98.3% in both, against 95.0% in the near-duplicate arm and 100.0% clean. Adding a competitor for a *different* tool costs it nothing, which is the arm's own internal control.

## What this supersedes, and what it does not

- **It supersedes the reading of "both together" in [`ablation-2026-08-31.md`](ablation-2026-08-31.md)**, whose numbers stay exactly as published. That table's third row is the degraded manifest; this arm is the two-defect measurement it was read as. The compounding claim survives the correction and gets stronger: −46.7 for the pair against −5.0 and −3.3 for the parts.
- **The published parts were never subsets of the published whole**, which composing this arm is what exposed — the linter refused the first composition because `description/duplicate` would not fire on it. `ablate-duplicate-tool` gives the competitor the *reference* description, so it duplicates a good one; the degraded manifest gives it the *vague* description, so it duplicates a bad one; and the degraded arm also thins `sum_by_category`'s argument description, which no ablation did. So "the parts sum to −8.3" compared three manifests that do not nest. This arm is a strict subset of the degraded variant instead, enforced by a test that asserts byte-identity for every shared tool and a lint result that cannot exceed the target's.
- **It does not measure the two defects separately in this configuration.** The single-defect arms use the competitor with the good description; a matching pair of single-defect arms against *this* competitor was not run, so −46.7 is still a joint effect, now with the neighbourhood excluded rather than the parts isolated.
- **The judge's own reasoning names the tiebreaker**, in the smoke trial recorded before the arm: with the two descriptions byte-identical, it chose on the *name* — *"`summarise_by_category` seems designed for summarizing by category (its name suggests it summarizes across categories)"* — and the name it preferred was the wrong one. A page whose two tools describe themselves identically is decided by something it probably did not think of as documentation.
- **Still one judge, one browser build, one page, one machine**, back-to-back sessions, n = 20 per tool per session.
